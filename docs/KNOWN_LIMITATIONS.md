# Known limitations

AHODO v0.3 is a discovery registry, not a corpus of underlying works or a representative sample of African humanities. The largest source contributes **3,461** records (**29.71%**). South African holding institutions account for **10,053** records. Repository records dominate because structured interfaces were available within reviewed scopes.

Subject geography is `not_established` for **11,496** records. Language is unstated for **4,950**; Shona is asserted on **0** active items and Ndebele on **1**. These counts do not show that such resources are absent elsewhere. Five items have no evidenced item-level URL.

Metadata licence is not established for **10,738** items. Public catalogue records do not imply that linked PDFs, text, images, audio or transcripts are available or reusable. Community governance is frequently not provided, and the release makes no blanket AI-training clearance claim. Scope and possible-duplicate assessments remain metadata-based. External source pages and terms may change after the recorded reviews.
