# Licensing and practical reuse

Read the [repository licensing notice](../LICENSE) alongside each item’s `rights_assessment` and the [public source manifest](../dataset/v0.3/source-manifest.json). Citation helps attribution and scholarship; citation alone does not grant reuse permission. AHODO-authored material and source-derived components have different rights.

AHODO-authored documentation, validator code, public schema, original examples and original compilation or explanatory expression are available for personal, educational and non-commercial research use under the current repository notice. Commercial republication or incorporation of AHODO-authored expression or code requires permission, unless separately licensed. AHODO makes no claim to own public facts, third-party metadata, independently obtained records or underlying works. Earlier permissions are not purportedly withdrawn.

The release has **10,738** records referencing metadata with licence `not_established`, **800** referencing CC0-1.0 metadata components and **112** referencing CC-BY-4.0 catalogue-text components. These are component-level findings, not blanket licences for every field or linked resource. [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) allows reuse of its assessed component, while [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) requires appropriate credit, a licence link and an indication of changes; other rights can still matter. The item attribution structure and source evidence give the applicable detail.

**Example A — CC0 museum catalogue metadata.** A record’s assessed CC0 component remains CC0 under the museum’s source evidence. AHODO does not convert that component into proprietary data, and the finding does not automatically extend to an image or artwork.

**Example B — repository metadata with licence `not_established`.** Inclusion in AHODO does not let AHODO license the source institution’s metadata. Assess the source terms and applicable law before further reuse.

**Example C — AHODO-authored schema or documentation.** Personal, educational and non-commercial research use is allowed by the current repository notice. Commercial republication or incorporation of AHODO-authored material requires permission from the relevant holder(s).

**Example D — underlying thesis, artwork or book.** A discovery record does not grant rights to the referenced work. Follow the original resource’s rights and any relevant community-governance conditions.

`not_provided` community-governance information is not consent or unrestricted use. A copyright/licence basis for a specified computational operation is not blanket AI permission. Cite AHODO and the relevant source institution or resource; respect any legally required source attribution separately.
