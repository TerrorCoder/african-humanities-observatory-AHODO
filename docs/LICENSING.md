# Licensing scope

The repository [MIT licence](../LICENSE) applies to AHODO-authored public documentation, schema and validation utility. It does not relicense third-party source metadata, catalogue text, images, books or other cultural material. AHODO does not assert one blanket licence for the normalized dataset compilation; each record and source-manifest resource assessment reports the evidence and status available for its component. In particular, `not_established` is not an open licence.

The African Minds catalogue fields are recorded under the stated CC BY 4.0 component assessment and require the applicable attribution and indication of changes. CC0 component findings at museum sources do not propagate automatically to unrelated media. Users should follow source-specific evidence and other applicable rights or governance conditions.
